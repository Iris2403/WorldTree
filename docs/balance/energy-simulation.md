# WORLD TREE: 7-Day Energy Simulation

> **Generated by `tools/balance/energy_sim.py`. Don't edit by hand; re-run the script.**
> Deterministic: no randomness, whole-second time steps. The full model is in the script header.

**Constants:** cap 100 Sap, +1 per 120 s while below the cap, regeneration only (no level-up, gift or chest Sap). The simulation starts at 00:00 on day 1 with a full bar and full generators. One tap every 4 s unless stated. Sessions end at Sap 0, at a generator stall, or after 20 min. Generators: Seed Pouch and Moonwell Spring, Lv1, 20 min refresh timer. 3 active orders, refilled from a repeating queue.

**Theoretical ceiling:** 86,400 s ÷ 120 s = 720 Sap per day, reachable only if Sap is never at the cap.

**Strategies:** *balanced* taps the generator with the most charges · *focused* taps only the Seed Pouch · *order-driven* taps for the oldest active order and switches to another active order's generator when that one is empty, never stockpiling · *strict-order* (sensitivity) only taps for the oldest order and stops when its generator is empty.

**Order demand patterns** (share of taps needed from the Seed Pouch): *even* 51% · *a-heavy* 73% · *capped-60* 60% · *capped-55* 55%. *capped-55* follows the GDD §6.2 demand rule.
