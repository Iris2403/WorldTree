# WORLD TREE: 7-Day Energy Simulation

> **Generated by `tools/balance/energy_sim.py`. Don't edit by hand; re-run the script.**
> Deterministic: no randomness, whole-second time steps. The model and assumptions are in the script header.

Constants: cap **100** Sap, **+1 per 120 s** while below the cap, regeneration only (no level-up, gift or chest Sap). Simulation starts at 00:00 on day 1 with a full bar of 100 Sap and full generators. One tap every N seconds (N = 4 unless stated). Sessions end at Sap 0, at a generator stall, or after 20 min. Generators: 20 min refill, tap policy "balanced" (worst case).

Theoretical regeneration ceiling: 86,400 s ÷ 120 s = **720 Sap per day**, reachable only if Sap is never at the cap. The reference row shows how close a 24/7 schedule gets.
